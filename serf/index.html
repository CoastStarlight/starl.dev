📖 How to Play
You are a serf in 1820s Russia. Survive 10 years to earn your freedom.
🌱
Spring — Plow & Plant
Press Plow & Plant to open the planting minigame. Match seeds to soil types. Every correct patch plants one field plot!
☀️
Summer — Wait & Tend
Hit Next Season to advance. Crops grow on their own. Rest to recover health, sell old grain, or save rubles for upgrades.
🍂
Autumn — Harvest Timing
Press Harvest to open the harvest minigame. Stop the swinging needle in the golden zone — then click each crop to collect it!
❄️
Winter — Pay or Suffer
The tax collector comes. You need rubles (5 + 2×year). Missing coins = beatings. Keep 4+ grain to avoid starving.
🏪
Market Upgrades
Buy land, seeds, tools and consumables to make farming easier and more profitable each year.
⚡
Random Events
Each season can bring surprise events — good or very bad. In early years: rats, illness, small windfalls. From year 3: thieves, soldiers, floods. From year 6: bandits stealing everything, barn fires, Cossack raids. Stay stocked up.
⚡
Harvest Minigame
A needle swings left and right. Click when it's inside the green zone to unlock a bonus multiplier. Then click each crop stalk to collect it. Miss the zone and get a penalty!
