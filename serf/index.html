📖 How to play
You are a serf in 1820s Russia. Survive 10 years to earn your freedom.
🌱
Spring — plow & plant
Press Plow & Plant to open the planting minigame. Match seeds to soil to fill more plots. Better performance = more grain at harvest.
☀️
Summer — wait & tend
Hit Next Season to advance. Crops grow on their own. Rest, sell old grain, or save rubles for market upgrades.
🍂
Autumn — harvest fast
Hit Harvest immediately. Blight can destroy crops (30% chance). Sell half your grain for rubles before winter taxes arrive.
❄️
Winter — pay or suffer
The tax collector comes. You need rubles (5 + 2×year). Missing coins = beatings = health loss. Keep 4+ grain to avoid starving.
🏪
Market upgrades
Buy more land, better seeds, a root cellar (stops grain rotting), a second ox (saves health plowing), or vodka to heal fast.
🌱
Planting minigame
Match seed to soil type: sandy 🟤→wheat, rich 🟫→sunflower, dark ⬛→carrot. Every correct patch plants a real plot. Be fast!
🐂 Plow & Plant
Match the right seed to each soil patch before time runs out. Every correct patch plants one field plot!
